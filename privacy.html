Privacy Policy — Project Snapshot for Jira

Version 1.2 — effective 2026-10-28

This policy explains what personal data FigFlows AB handles in connection with the app Project Snapshot for Jira (the "App"), why, and what your rights are. It is written to meet the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Swedish Data Protection Act (2018:218).

The short version: the App itself sends us nothing. The only personal data we ever process is what you send us when you contact support.

2. What the App does with your Jira data

The App runs inside Jira Cloud on Atlassian's Forge platform. When you start an export, the App reads the Jira project you selected through Atlassian's own API, builds ZIP files in your web browser's memory, and saves them to your computer.

  • We do not receive any of that data. The App has no server of its own, no database, no file storage and no connection to any host other than Atlassian's.
  • We do not use cookies, analytics, tracking pixels, local storage or session storage.
  • We do not know who you are, which projects you export, or when. Atlassian tells us only aggregate licence information (number of installs and the customer's site name for billing).
  • The App keeps a small snapshot log inside your Atlassian site. Since version 1.1, after each finished export the App stores one record in Atlassian's Forge hosted storage: the project's id, key and name; the export mode; the time; the Atlassian account ID of the person who ran it; counts (issues, attachments, bytes, ZIP parts, errors); the SHA-256 hash of the manifest; whether sample mode was on; and the JQL used. It never contains issue content, comments or attachments. The only personal data in it is that account ID. The log is stored by Atlassian in the same location as your Jira site, is readable only by the App inside your site, and is not accessible to us. At most 50 records per project are kept; older ones are deleted automatically. When the App is uninstalled, Atlassian deletes the log under its Forge retention rules (soft-deleted, then destroyed on Atlassian's standard schedule).
  • Since version 1.3 the App also keeps your snapshot policy in the same storage. If a Jira administrator switches the policy on, the App stores the rule (how old a snapshot may be, which projects to ignore, which project receives reminders and, if the administrator chose to be assigned, that administrator's Atlassian account ID), a summary of the last weekly check, the list of your Jira projects (id, key and name — so the weekly check knows which projects exist) and the key of each reminder task it created. Names and keys of projects are the only new data; still no issue content, comments or attachments. This data is stored and deleted under the same Atlassian rules as the snapshot log.
  • The App can create reminder tasks in your Jira — and nothing else. When the policy is on and a reminder project is chosen, the App creates one Jira task per project that is behind the rule ("Snapshot due: KEY", labelled project-snapshot-reminder, with the reason and a link to the export page). That is the only thing the App ever writes to Jira; it never changes, comments on or deletes anything. The tasks are ordinary issues in your own Jira. The App sends no e-mail; if the assignee receives one, it comes from Jira's own notification settings.

Because the App does not transfer any Jira data to us, we are not a data processor for your Jira data, including the snapshot log, the policy and the project list. That data stays under your organisation's control and Atlassian's, under the terms you have with Atlassian. The exported ZIP files are on your own computer and are your responsibility from that moment on.

4. Where the data is and who else sees it

Support e-mail is handled by our e-mail provider, Microsoft 365 (Microsoft Ireland Operations Ltd), acting as our processor under its standard data-processing terms. Servers are in the EU (Microsoft EU Data Boundary).

If our provider transfers data outside the EEA, the transfer is covered by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and, where applicable, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. Microsoft stores and processes our mailbox data within the EU Data Boundary; any residual transfer relies on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses in the Microsoft Products and Services Data Protection Addendum.

We do not sell, rent or share personal data with anyone else. We may disclose it if a Swedish court or authority requires it by law.

There are no sub-processors for App data, because the App sends us no data. The snapshot log is held by Atlassian as part of the Forge platform under your own agreement with Atlassian.

5. Your rights

Under GDPR you can ask us to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you (Art. 15)
  • correct it (Art. 16)
  • delete it (Art. 17)
  • restrict how we use it (Art. 18)
  • receive it in a portable format (Art. 20)
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21)

Write to andre@figflows.com. We answer within one month (Art. 12(3)). We may ask you to confirm your identity, usually by replying from the e-mail address the request concerns. There is no charge.
