Terms of Use (EULA) — Project Snapshot for Jira

Recommendation first: use Atlassian's standard agreement

Atlassian offers a free, pre-drafted Standard End User Agreement (the Bonterms Standard End User Agreement v1.0, CC BY-ND) that any Marketplace partner can adopt instead of writing their own EULA. It is adopted from the app version's Links tab in the partner console by answering Yes to "Use a standard agreement as your End User License Agreement?", then filling in the Provider-Specific Terms field. Over 350 apps use it.

  • Agreement text: https://www.atlassian.com/licensing/marketplace/end-user-agreement-v1
  • Atlassian's adoption guide: https://developer.atlassian.com/platform/marketplace/list-customizable-end-user-agreement/
  • Bonterms instructions: https://bonterms.com/standard/end-user-agreement-v1/instructions/

We recommend adopting it. For a one-developer company it is simpler, it is already familiar to enterprise procurement teams, and Atlassian maintains it. The Standard Agreement already contains warranty disclaimers, limitation of liability, and a framework for the customer's data. All we need to add are the Provider-Specific Terms below. Note that updates to the Standard Agreement are opt-in; we do not move to new versions automatically.

Atlassian's adoption page also says a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) is required "if you process personal data under GDPR". The App does not transfer any customer data to us, so we are not a processor of App data and no DPA is needed for the App. See data-handling.md, section 7, for the exact statement to give customers who ask.

The one thing it can create in Jira is a "Snapshot due" reminder task, and only when a Jira administrator has switched the snapshot policy on and chosen a project for those tasks.
